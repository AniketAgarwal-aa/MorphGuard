# ONOT Ground-Truth Review

The local ONOT package contains digital and print-scan acquisition variants and three cosine-threshold text lists. The lists identify threshold subsets, not image-level genuine/morphed labels. Folder and filename conventions expose candidate subject identifiers and acquisition information, but do not by themselves establish class ground truth. Official context: [ONOT documentation](https://miatbiolab.csr.unibo.it/icao-synthetic-dataset/). No ONOT record is automatically approved as bona fide.
